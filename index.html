Text message terms for Eddie Landesberg's personal assistant

Who sends

Eddie Landesberg, an individual, through a personal assistant program.

Who receives, and how they opt in

Only a person who asked for it and texted the number first. No one is added any other way. The first reply to that first text says who is sending, how often to expect messages, that message and data rates may apply, and how to stop or get help.

What is sent

Replies to that person's texts and reminders they asked for. No marketing.

How often

A few messages a day at most.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Stop and help

Reply STOP at any time to end messages. Reply HELP for help, or email edward.landesberg@gmail.com.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and message content are not sold or shared with anyone for marketing. Opt-in information is never shared with third parties.
