Privacy Policy

This policy covers the text messages sent by Eddie Landesberg, an individual, through his personal assistant program.

What is collected

The mobile number of a person who texts the assistant's number, the fact and time that they opted in by texting first, and the content of the messages exchanged.

How it is used

Only to reply to that person's texts and to send reminders they asked for. It is not used for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages pass through the phone carriers and the messaging provider that deliver them, and are shared with no one else.

Stopping, and removing your data

Reply STOP at any time to end messages. To ask for your number and messages to be deleted, email edward.landesberg@gmail.com.
